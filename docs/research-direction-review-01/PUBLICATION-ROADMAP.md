## E5 and SS treatment

E5 may motivate measurement validity and SS may motivate end-to-end maintenance,
but complete public, corrected supporting evidence is not available in this
Core review. Do not import private numerical results or old uncorrected oracle
claims, recalculate hypotheses, or describe the history as uniformly positive.
Treatment availability/adoption, valid implementation, oracle validity and
final correctness must remain separate. A separately authorized manuscript
team can cite only the versions it can independently substantiate and disclose
corrections. Nothing here revises their frozen outcomes or Paper 1/2.

## Recommended writing order and research identity

1. Package the **existing-code maintenance and authority-bound evidence** system
   story with P33–P36 as bounded cases and P38 as an explicit counterweight.
   Reuse current artifacts, avoid new feature construction.
2. Only if independently authorized, test the single D measurement hypothesis
   in [NEXT-EXPERIMENT-SPEC](NEXT-EXPERIMENT-SPEC.md). If conventional state-aware
   evaluation matches, write the lesson as a case/replication contribution,
   not a newly superior evaluator. B remains the only secondary option.
3. Mainstream-language evidence is conditional, not another automatic study.
   No next-paper promise, model procurement or expanded task corpus follows.

Research positioning supported now: **engineering and empirically studying the
boundary between a coding agent's requested operations, actual program changes,
publication authority and independent validation**. Useful outcomes include
negative results and clear non-claims. Unsupported positioning includes
replacing conventional languages, intrinsically correct agents, AGI-required
languages, proof-carrying safety without proofs, or generic superiority over
CodeStruct/Serena/OpenRewrite/Git.

If no narrow question survives strong baseline and novelty review, stop new
Agent mechanism experiments, maintain the reproducible system and publish what
was actually learned. A principled stop is preferable to relabeling an old
ledger or tuning another difficulty distribution.
