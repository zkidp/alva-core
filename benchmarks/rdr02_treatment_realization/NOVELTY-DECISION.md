# INCONCLUSIVE_EVIDENCE_LIMITATION

This is the sole primary RDR-02 disposition. There is **no observed incremental
measurement advantage** on the accessible evidence, but the full version-bound
chain cannot be independently reconstructed to the stronger receipt level for
every unit. Do not present this as a statistically validated negative result,
a universal impossibility theorem, or as NO_OBSERVED_INCREMENTAL_VALUE with all
four chains sufficient.

E0 and E1 agree on every reported substantive proposition and classification;
actual disagreements = **0**. E0 has the same constructor/dispatch records,
observer hashes, commit outcomes, final authority and executed-test observations.
AIR has not supplied an E1-only measurement capability. Naming more stages is
not a new measurement ability. No baseline is weakened and no extra information
is given to E1.

The remaining limits are explicit in EVIDENCE-AVAILABILITY and OBSERVATIONS:
U2 lacks per-operation staged-target observations and shared bridge call IDs;
U2/U4 have co-recorded final files/evaluation, not a separately echoed exact
tested-revision receipt. U3's staging has no independent behavioral evaluation
and remains NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_EVALUATED. U1 is non-model engineering evidence.
Original archived outcomes are not rescored or disputed by these qualifications.

## Direction decision

- **Do not advance Direction D as a new measurement mechanism.** There is no
  positive discriminator and incomplete evidence is not an advantage.
- Do not claim its strongest four-unit falsifier was fully discharged. Preserve
  the distinction between observed parity and unresolved identity strength.
- Do not collect more tasks, weaken E0, demand reruns, tune criteria, start a
  model study, or rebrand the same mechanism. Missing historical evidence stays
  missing. No follow-up research is authorized by this node.
- Proceed only with the requested **publication handoff**: an ALVA systems and
  P33–P36 locality/publication case study, with P37's bounded engineering pool,
  P38's original NO_OBSERVED_INCREMENTAL_VALUE and this limited RDR-02 analysis.
  Direction D is not its main innovation.

RDR-01 remains unchanged as the decision/design that authorized this test.
This result is its scoped follow-through, not an alteration of its historical
recommendation. The separate Reviewer can choose to close the D line outright;
this report does not turn evidence limitation into a request for new paid runs.
