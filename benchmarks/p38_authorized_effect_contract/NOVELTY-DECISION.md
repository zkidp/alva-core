# NO_OBSERVED_INCREMENTAL_VALUE

This disposition concerns the tested AEC hypothesis relative to strong source
diff plus tests, not the usefulness of ALVA as a whole. Four synthetic cases
are too few to establish a general negative result or a priority claim.

**RQ1 — Increment over P34:** Yes, at the level of the stronger policy
composition. C1 reproduces a real structural-policy blind spot: actual Guard
permission, actual AIR commit and fresh authority coexist with an independently
observed protected caller regression. Adding the registered behavior test
rejects it. C4 shows an explicit proof requirement cannot be replaced by tests.
These are policy additions beyond P34, not newly discovered failures in P34's
stated function-body guarantee. D is only an offline simulated gate here.

**RQ2 — Increment over strong Git Diff + Tests:** None observed. A and D make
the same four decisions, with the same finite behavior evidence and proof
requirement. The protected regression is already visible to ordinary tests;
the UNKNOWN rule can be enforced by any trusted pipeline. Extra potential
impact explanations supply provenance, not additional authorization power in
these cases. B also matches on all three sample-governed cases. Do not promote
this integration into a new correctness mechanism or broad scientific result.

**RQ3 — Is authoritative AIR essential?** It gives useful reproducible body
identity, shared-structure witnesses, staging and fresh publication evidence.
Those are concrete engineering advantages for this study. No tested decision
requires an irreplaceable AIR feature: source hashes/Git commits, an adequate
parser, isolation and trusted evaluation can bind the same policies. C3's
native shared-parent mutation is representation-specific, but its final
unauthorized declaration changes are also detected by the source baseline.
Crash/concurrent publication equivalence between architectures was not tested.

**RQ4 — Transfer:** Trusted intent, three-way decisions, candidate-bound
evidence and explicit strength requirements transfer conceptually to Python
and TypeScript. ALVA body roots, public MCP constructors, AIR publication and
this direct-call collector do not transfer unchanged. Mainstream symbol/AST
tools such as CodeStruct, Serena or OpenRewrite could supply structural
evidence. Dynamic resolution, reflection, aliases, external libraries and I/O
make impact completeness harder; unresolved effects must remain UNKNOWN.
No Python/TypeScript implementation or soundness claim is delivered.

Recommendation: shrink AEC to an experimental Host policy/evidence format and
reuse strong existing checks. Do not justify Core expansion, a new effect
engine, model experiment or P39 from this result. Any future hypothesis would
need a prospectively registered case where equivalent evidence plus a strong
source baseline demonstrably cannot provide the same decision or provenance
property, with costs and assumptions made explicit. No such case is established
here. Prior art review is bounded; global originality remains unverified.
